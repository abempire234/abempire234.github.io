Episode 01: The Beginning

This is the story of a king. How did he become a king? In the beginning, he faced many problems and hardships, but eventually he became a great ruler known as "The Winder King."

Young Winder is playing with his elder brother and elder sister. While playing, they accidentally enter a cave. Winder and his brother manage to come out, but their elder sister does not.

Many years pass. Winder grows up and wonders whether his sister is still alive.

One day, soldiers call him to the military headquarters. The army of Hax Light is approaching their border.

Winder leads his soldiers into battle. A giant monster appears with a massive shield and crossbow. Winder orders his soldiers to activate their shields and fight together.

Eventually, the war comes to a temporary halt. But everyone knows Hax Light will return.

Bulky Beast Returns Home

Winder returns to the cave where his sister was trapped. He tells the Commander that Hax Light wants to take over the kingdom.

Winder reveals the secret of the legendary Golden Hold Sword, a weapon said to have the power to destroy or create a planet.

Winder orders the evacuation of his people and activates the Safety Protocol.

Meanwhile, the danger grows. Winder's older brother, Bulky Beast, returns to help him face the enemy.

I See a Dragon!!

Winder faces a dangerous battle against Hax Knight's forces. Just as he is about to be defeated, two dragons arrive from the sky.

One of the dragons approaches Winder. He discovers that the dragon is his older brother, Bulky Beast.

Bulky Beast explains that he travelled to the Dragon Empire and trained to understand his hidden power.

He reveals that he can transform between human and dragon forms.

The Hidden Betrayal

The two brothers return to the mysterious cave, hoping to open its sealed gate.

They meet the Commander, who claims that everyone is safe. But Bulky Beast has seen something suspicious.

At Hax Knight's headquarters, the Commander reveals that he is secretly working for the enemy.

Bulky Beast tells Winder the truth. Winder is shocked to learn that his closest friend has betrayed him.
